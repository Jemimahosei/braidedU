# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: Cornell students, mainly Black women, who need braids and want to book a student braider without chasing DMs; and student braiders who take appointments between classes.
Second audience (confirmed): recruiters and hiring managers who open this project from Jemimah's resume and judge it in about thirty seconds. The site must read as a considered product to someone who never books braids.

## Product Purpose
braidedU is a campus marketplace for braiding services. Clients find a braider, see the real price and duration up front, and request an appointment. Braiders list styles, manage requests and build a reputation through reviews. Success: a booking made in one place instead of a pile of DMs.

## Positioning
Price, duration and availability are public before anyone asks. Built by a Cornell student for students, a rebuild of the Kormo Mela Devpost project with a braiding focus.

## Operating Context
Web app (Vite, React, TypeScript, hash routing) on a Django REST API. Live demo currently on Render's free tier; Vercel migration planned. Pages: home, browse, braider profile, signup/login, client and braider dashboards.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Browse and rank braiders by style, date, campus area and budget. Booking requests with accept, decline, complete, cancel and reviews. Demo accounts exist; all seeded braiders are labeled "Demo profile". No payments. No uploads of photos exist yet.

## Brand Commitments
Name: braidedU (formerly Kormo Mela). Plain, warm voice, made by a student for students. Binding (confirmed by Jemimah): no fake people, prices stay visible up front, the name and tone stay, and Ghanaian roots show in the look.

## Evidence on Hand
Seeded demo braiders, listings and prices from `seed_demo`. No real photos, testimonials or reviews. Do not fabricate people, faces, testimonials or statistics.

## Product Principles
- Price and time before promise.
- A booking is one shared record, not a thread of DMs.
- Show craft without faking people.
- Warm and specific beats polished and generic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion respected. No product-specific standard was stated.
